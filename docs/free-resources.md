# Free resources — candidate list for the Academy

Researched 2026-11-19. Nothing here is on the site yet: this is the long list
to select from. Status column says how far each claim was checked.

- **Verified** = I opened the official page this session and read it.
- **Search only** = seen in search results from the official domain; not opened.
- **From Todoist** = a link Avrel saved; destination not opened (Google Drive).

Language: EN or FR. 4TUNHub is francophone-first, so FR items are flagged.

## 0. Read first: the SolidProfessor Drive folders

Todoist holds two Google Drive folders labelled "SolidProfessor" (main folder and
"7. SolidWorks"), described as "CSWE study material".

SolidProfessor is a **paid** subscription (its own site says members pay from
$49 a month). A Drive folder of its course files is almost certainly someone
else's copy of paid material. **Update 2026-11-19:** I opened it. It is a "Download all" folder of
SolidProfessor's 2020 course catalogue (about 17 course folders and a
"Courses Catalog.txt"), shared with the owner hidden. That is a copy of the
paid catalogue, not something SolidProfessor published. It stays off the site.
The Academy page links SolidProfessor's own site instead, labelled Paid.

The public page is `/academy/free-resources`, built from
`src/lib/free-resources.ts`. This file remains the longer working list.

**Do not link it from the public Academy.** It is a copyright and reputation risk
for a site that sells trust. Keep it as private study material for Avrel only.
Section 1 gives legitimate free replacements that cover the same ground.

The same caution applies to the **Mohamed Zedan Drive** ("field of mechanics")
until someone opens it and checks what is in it.

## Academy restructure and previews (2026-11-24)

Decided after a look at Microsoft Learn, web.dev, MIT OCW, Ansys Innovation,
freeCodeCamp, Brilliant and SolidProfessor (Onshape and Khan returned nothing:
their pages render in JavaScript).

- The Academy page now goes: hero, cohort, **Start here** (three cards by goal,
  each with a preview), sessions already taught, list, enrolment. Removed
  because they described things that do not exist yet: the course catalogue,
  the "How you learn" block (online courses, mentoring) and the instructor
  block (the founder page has it). `src/lib/academy.ts` is gone.
- The library moved from `/academy/ls-dyna` to **`/academy/youtube`**, named
  "YouTube library". The old address, with or without a language prefix and
  for every episode, redirects permanently (`next.config.ts`).
  The file URLs under `/library/ls-dyna/` did not move.
- **Previews** live in `public/images/previews/` and are always ours: the CSP
  allows only `'self'` images, and a third-party image would tell that third
  party who is reading. Sources: a YouTube thumbnail (the CSWA walk-through and
  one episode), page 1 of two public PDFs, and a screenshot of our own page.
  `preview` and `facts` on a resource are optional; facts are only ones
  checked (the PDFs' page counts, read from the files: 80 and 71).
- Five cards on the list have a preview. The rest stay text, on purpose: the
  page is read on slow phones.
- "Academy" stays the name. Renaming it "Learn" is open: the evidence was that
  four of the seven sites use the word, but the cohort and its shared links
  carry "Academy".

- 2026-11-24: added SOLIDWORKS' own Getting Started tutorials to the SolidWorks group (open page, four lessons, 2 h 30; read that day; 2024 edition, the 2026 address does not exist).

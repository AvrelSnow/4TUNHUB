## Rules for the week

1. **Never post the card without the caption.** The card carries the
   facts so the caption doesn't have to; a card alone gets scrolled past.
2. **Reply to every comment within the hour.** On LinkedIn the first
   hour decides how far a post travels, and a question answered in public
   is an application from someone else reading it.
3. **The screenshots are the bottleneck**, not the form. Expect people to
   apply and forget them; the confirmation page asks, and a WhatsApp
   nudge on the 9th to anyone who hasn't sent them is worth more than
   another post.
4. **When a fact changes, change it in the script and regenerate.** The
   whole set rebuilds in about a minute. A card that outlives its facts is
   worse than no card — and `SEATS_LEFT` is the one that will go stale
   fastest.
5. **The ambassadors are the middle week, not an interruption of it.**
   Their ten reveals are ten networks reached; 4TUNHub reposting each one
   is what makes saying yes worth it for them.

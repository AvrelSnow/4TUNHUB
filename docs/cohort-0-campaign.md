# Cohort 0 — the launch campaign

> Written 20 September 2026. Facts of record: `docs/launch-plan.md`.
> Cards: `node scripts/make-postcards.mjs` → `./postcards`.

Seven cards, one a day, from launch to the deadline. Each one is posted
with the caption below it. The cards carry the facts; the captions carry
the voice.

**Sizes.** `-post.png` is 1080×1350 — LinkedIn, Facebook, the feed.
`-story.png` is 1080×1920 — WhatsApp status, Shorts, Reels.

**Every link goes to** `4tunhub.com/en/academy/cohort-0`. On LinkedIn, put
it in the first comment rather than the post: the feed suppresses posts
that send people off-platform.

## Rules for the week

1. **Never post the card without the caption.** The card carries the
   facts so the caption doesn't have to; a card alone gets scrolled past.
2. **Reply to every comment within the hour.** On LinkedIn the first
   hour decides how far a post travels, and a question answered in public
   is an application from someone else reading it.
3. **The screenshots are the bottleneck**, not the form. Expect people to
   apply and forget them; the confirmation page asks, and a WhatsApp
   nudge on the 30th to anyone who hasn't sent them is worth more than
   another post.
4. **When a fact changes, change it in the script and regenerate.** Seven
   cards take about a minute to rebuild. A card that outlives its facts is
   worse than no card.

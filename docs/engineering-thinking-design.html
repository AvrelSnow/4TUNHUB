3 · Rewards: three moments

MomentEarned byWhat happens
StampSeen: lessons finished. Practised: their checks passed without showing the working. Proven: the bracket steps that card teaches are cleared (cards 1–4)It presses onto the card
AssemblyA card reaches PractisedIts piece drops onto the bracket
Load testAll 8 bracket numbers clearedYour own load and thickness: stress colours computed along the plate, sag drawn enlarged and labelled so, then the stamp

The trade-off: this is calmer than “a lot of effects”. Each reward means something, and there is less to build and break. All motion switches off for people who ask for reduced motion.

# P006 - Position at MMC

**{{student_name}} · Version {{challenge_version}} · Run {{run_no}}**

A drawing calls out a hole of diameter **{{D_min}} to {{D_max}} mm**, with a
position tolerance of diameter **{{T}} mm at MMC** (the modifier M in the
feature control frame), to datums A, B and C.

The inspection report for one part says:

- the hole's **actual size** is diameter **{{d}} mm**;
- its axis is **{{dx}} mm** off the true position in X and **{{dy}} mm** off in Y.

Give, in **millimetres to three decimals**:

1. the hole's **maximum material condition** (its MMC size);
2. its **virtual condition**;
3. the **bonus tolerance** this part earns;
4. the **position tolerance zone** this part is allowed (a diameter);
5. the **position error** the part has (the diameter of the circle around the
   true position that holds the axis);
6. the **verdict**: type **1** to accept the part, **2** to reject it for
   position, **3** to reject it for size.

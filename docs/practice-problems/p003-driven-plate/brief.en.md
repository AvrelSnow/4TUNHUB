![Dimensioned schematic of the plate (its length is not given)](drawing.svg)

A flat plate, **{{W}} mm** wide and **{{t}} mm** thick, carries a row of
**{{n}}** round holes of diameter **{{d}} mm** on its centre line. The holes
are **{{s}} mm** apart. The first and last holes sit **{{e}} mm** from the
plate's two ends.

Do not type the plate's length. In SOLIDWORKS, create global variables
for e, s and n, and drive the length with an equation so that it follows the
number of holes.

Material: density **{{rho}} kg/m3**.

1. What is the plate's **length** (mm)?
2. What is its **mass** (g)?
3. Now increase the number of holes by **{{dn}}** and let the part rebuild.
   What is the **new mass** (g)?

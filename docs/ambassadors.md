# Cohort 0 ambassadors

> Decided 2026-09-20. Ten people, four weeks, ten of the twenty seats.
> Campaign: `docs/cohort-0-campaign.md`. Dates: `docs/launch-plan.md`.

## What this is, and what it is not

An ambassador is **not** a volunteer promoting something they are not part
of. Those programmes die in two weeks: ten are recruited, two post, eight
go quiet, and the founder ends up chasing people to do him a favour.

An ambassador here is **an engineer who wants the CSWA, is given a seat in
Cohort 0, and tells their own circle what they are learning while they
learn it.** The daily post is not a favour. It is them talking about their
own week — which is also better content than anything 4TUN Hub could
write about itself.

Ten of the twenty seats are theirs. It costs nothing (the same twenty
exercises get marked either way), it makes the offer worth saying yes to,
and it makes the ten remaining seats visibly scarce.

## Who to look for, in priority order

1. **They already run something.** A class WhatsApp group, a campus club,
   a page, a set of classmates who listen to them. One person with a real
   group of 200 engineering students beats five with 3,000 scattered
   followers.
2. **They want the CSWA themselves.** Getting something, not doing a favour.
3. **They have already done something unasked** — for the SWUG, for
   classmates, for you. Past behaviour is the only reliable predictor.
4. **They answer within 24 hours.** Someone slow during selection is gone
   by week two.
5. **Spread.** Not ten from IUC. Douala, Dschang, ENSET, a polytechnic,
   and one or two outside Cameroon — the REM's readers are not all here.

Where the thirty names come from: students taught (300+), active members
of the Douala City SWUG (483), REM readers who reply, the WhatsApp
community, and the first people to apply for a seat.

## How they are chosen: the task, not an interview

No open call. An open call for "ambassadors" attracts people who want the
title. **Invite thirty directly**, then let one task do the selecting.

> **The invite** (WhatsApp or LinkedIn DM, sent 22–24 Sep)
>
> [Name] — I'm running a free CSWA bootcamp in October with the Douala
> City SOLIDWORKS User Group. Eight live evenings, and everyone who
> finishes gets a free exam voucher.
>
> I'm keeping ten of the twenty seats for people who will carry it to
> their own circle. You came to mind because [the specific reason — the
> group they run, the thing they did].
>
> It's four weeks, about two minutes a day and fifteen on a weekend. You
> get the seat and the voucher, your name and photo on the site, a signed
> letter of recommendation at the end, and first refusal on a free seat in
> the paid cohort in January.
>
> If you're in, say yes and I'll send you one thing to do on Friday. That
> one thing is how I pick.

Then, on launch day:

> Here it is. Share this post in your group and on LinkedIn, and send me
> the screenshots. **You have 48 hours.**

**The ten who do it are the ambassadors.** No interviews, no judgement
calls, no awkward rejections — the people who did not reply have answered
for free. If more than ten complete it, take the ten with the widest
spread of institutions and keep the rest as the waiting list.

## What they do

**Daily — two minutes.** Repost the day's card to their status and to one
group they are in.

**Weekly — fifteen minutes.** One post in *their own words*, not a
copy-paste. Once the cohort starts, that post is simply what they built
that week. Plus a screenshot bundle of what they posted, sent to the
ambassador group. The bundle is the accountability loop; without it the
programme has no pulse.

**Answer questions in their circle**, and pass on the ones they cannot.

**Not their job:** teaching, choosing students, touching applications,
speaking for 4TUN Hub, or promising anyone anything. Anything official
comes to Fortune.

**Four-week term**, renewable for Cohort 1. Open-ended volunteering dies
quietly; a term with an end date gets finished.

**If someone goes quiet for a week:** one message. If they go quiet again,
the seat goes to the waiting list, said plainly and without drama at the
start so it is never a surprise. The standard is the same one the cohort
itself is held to.

## What they get — none of it costs cash

| | |
|---|---|
| A seat in Cohort 0 | Plus the free CSWA voucher with it |
| Their name and photo on the site | An Ambassadors section, real credibility both ways |
| A signed letter of recommendation | From a CSWP engineer and lecturer. On a student CV here, worth real money |
| The week's best post reposted | By 4TUN Hub *and* the SWUG, to 483 members. Their audience grows — this is why someone with a group says yes |
| First refusal on Cohort 1 | A free seat in the paid one |

## The reveal: the announcement *is* the distribution

Each ambassador gets their own card — photo, name, school — and **posts it
themselves**. Two a day over five days, 29 September to 3 October. Ten
cards, ten networks, ten people proud enough to share something with
their own face on it. That reaches further than anything posted from one
account, and it is a week of campaign rather than overhead before it.

To generate them: add each person to `AMBASSADORS` in
`scripts/make-postcards.mjs` (name, role, path to a JPEG or PNG portrait)
and run the script.
